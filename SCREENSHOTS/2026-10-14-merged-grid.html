Live activity merged into the Tracked Repositories grid (2026-10-14)

Both shots were taken mid-analysis of punkouter26/PoRepoLineTracker, with the same seeded demo data.

  • The "Live activity" card above the grid is gone. Each running job's live tally, commits/s, ETA and elapsed time now show in its own row's Status cell.
  • The Streaming / Reconnecting indicator moved next to the "Tracked Repositories" heading and only appears while a job is running.
  • Before, a job for a repository the grid hadn't loaded yet appeared only in the panel. Now the page reloads its list on that job's first frame, so the job gets a row.
  • Dropped from the old panel: the 5-stage rail (the step badge plus "Step 3/4" covers it), the discovered file-type chips, and the green finish flash.
